Tchoukaillon one-row mancala, solved

A row of pits numbered 1, 2, 3, … away from the store. Harvesting pit i sows its stones one per pit toward the store, so the move is legal only when pit i holds exactly i stones (the last one lands in the store). Pocket every stone to win. The solver is exact: if a win exists, the winning move is always the smallest harvestable pit, and for every number of stones there is exactly one winning board. With hints on, the winning move is marked ✓ and every other legal move ✗.
